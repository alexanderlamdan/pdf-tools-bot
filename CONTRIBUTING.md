## Access Conditions

All authorized reviewers must:

1. Sign a **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)** before access is granted
2. Agree to the terms in `LICENSE` and `SECURITY.md`
3. Not retain copies of source code after the review period
4. Report any discovered vulnerabilities per `SECURITY.md`
5. Not share, publish, or discuss the codebase with third parties
